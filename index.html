What is Orbital Launcher?

Your home screen

It's what you see when you unlock your phone: your apps, your wallpaper, and things like the clock and the weather.

Made your way

Orbital is an app that gives you a new one. Put your favorite apps right under your thumb, and choose the layout, colors and style from dozens of themes.

Nothing else changes

Your other apps, photos and settings stay as they are. Go back to your old home screen whenever you like, in your phone's settings.

Free · For Android phones, Android 8.0 and later · No account needed

New in Orbital

Two new ways to lay out home

Press, hold, tap and swipe: both of these work right here.

Orbit Pad New

Hold the pad where your thumb rests and every app orbits around it. Slide onto one and let go to open it. Your dock's apps sit closest, then the ones you use most, then everything else.

Showcase New

Big tiles in a row, recent apps first, over a backdrop in the color of the app you're on. Shelves for recents, games and music, quick actions under each tile, and it works with a game controller.
