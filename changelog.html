What's new

The latest improvements to Orbital, newest first.

Coming next Next update

  • Orbit Pad. A new home layout: hold the pad where your thumb rests and every app orbits around it, then slide onto one and let go. It also comes as a dock style and a widget.
  • Showcase. A new home layout of large tiles over a backdrop in the color of the app you're on, with shelves, quick actions and widget bays. Works with a game controller.
  • Five more languages: Spanish, Portuguese, German, French and Hindi, with Orbital Assistant in each.
  • The theme store gets a theme of the week, drops, seasonal themes, browsing by mood and color, and try before you apply.
  • Match my wallpaper, a free theme that takes its color from your wallpaper, and a wallpaper gallery.
  • Dock Menu, a small pill under the status bar that opens into a card of your apps.
  • The app drawer gets tabs, its own edit mode, two to ten columns and sorting by category.

0.10 Latest

  • Themes grouped by style, each with a dock and layout to match.
  • Bring your home screen from another launcher, with a preview first and Undo after.
  • Gestures: swipes, double tap and two-finger swipes, each set to what you like.
  • The letter dock fits its apps, sits where you choose and makes room on the page.
  • Free roam carries widgets across pages and has a map for moving folders.
  • Resize widgets from any edge, and the calendar fills the space it's given.
  • Edit mode's buttons are grouped, with Done last.

0.9

  • "Add to Home screen" works. Shortcuts and widgets offered by Chrome, Maps, Contacts and other apps land right on the page in front of you.
  • Orbital Assistant at the top of your app list. It understands what you type as you type it: an errand shows what it will do with a button to do it, a sum shows its answer, and an app name finds the app.
  • Free roam moves freely, with a small guide showing which folder you're in and where you are on its canvas.
  • Carry widgets between pages. Hold one at the edge of the screen while arranging and the next page turns to meet it.
  • Type a slash from any app to open Orbital Assistant on phones with a keyboard.
  • More apps per row on an open foldable.
  • Cancel is back in edit mode, and it asks before discarding your changes.
  • Keyboard phones can keep the home screen upright and choose what typing does.

0.8

  • Folders you can walk into on Free roam. Spread two fingers to open a folder or start one, and pinch to step back out.
  • Drawer mode for the dock. The dock becomes an alphabet, with the apps for each letter just above it.
  • Widgets on the Drawer layout, above or below your apps.
  • Shortcut marks for Orbital Assistant: @ to text, + for an event, - for a note, * for a reminder, ^ to open an app, # to call and & for a to-do.
  • A Command prompt look for the assistant.
  • Orbital as your phone's assistant, opening over any app.
  • Swipe down can open your notifications.
  • A snappier home screen when pages land, when edit mode opens and when you move through folders.

0.7

  • Holding the background goes straight into edit mode, with Widgets, the clock, the wallpaper and Settings close at hand.
  • Picking up widgets is quicker, and a widget lands exactly where you let go.
  • Two pages side by side when you open a foldable.
  • Eight named colors, each with a single slider.
  • Rows in the app drawer: an alphabetical list, several apps to a row.
  • Matrix, with glyphs falling behind every card.
  • Every animation follows your Motion setting.
