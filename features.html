The dock

Every dock style

Pick the style that suits your hand, and keep it with you inside your other apps.

The orbit

Apps ride an ellipse along the edge of the screen. Turn it and the next app comes round to the front, right under your thumb.

Up to three orbits ✦

Add a second and third ring for the apps you open a little less often. Drag outward from any icon to bring them within reach.

Flat row

A familiar row of apps along the edge, with or without their names. Simple, and always in the same place.

Container

A compact card of apps that scrolls to show more, set to the left, center or right. The rest of the edge can show the time, your battery, quick controls or frosted glass.

Drawer mode ✦

Turn the dock into an alphabet. Spin to a letter and its apps appear just above it, so every app on your phone is one flick away.

Over other apps

Keep the dock nearby while you use other apps. It rests as a slim handle at the edge and slides out as soon as you reach for it. Always free.

Home layouts

Choose how your home screen works

Three layouts, each with its own way of getting around. Switch whenever you like: your pages stay exactly as you arranged them, ready for when you come back.

Traditional pages

Swipe between pages as usual, and grow them in any direction: left, right, up or down. Name each page and place widgets exactly where you want them.

Free roam ✦

One open canvas that scrolls in every direction. Spread two fingers to open a folder or start a new one, and pinch to step back out. A small compass shows where you are.

Drawer ✦

Every app right on the home screen, as a grid or a list. Hold the background to add widgets above or below your apps.

Icons

Every icon in the shape you choose

Give every app the same outline, or leave them as their makers drew them. Then choose how they're colored: their own colors, flattened, or recolored to match your theme. Icon packs from the Play Store work too. Pick a shape below to see it.

✦ Choosing your own icon shape, color style and icon pack is part of Orbital Premium. Every free theme comes with a shape and style of its own.

Honeycomb

Hexagon icons fit together edge to edge, row upon row, on your pages and in the app list.

Tiles

Each app fills its square with its name across the foot. Live tiles turn over to show what's coming up.

Orbital Assistant

Type what you need. Orbital takes care of it.

Ask Orbital understands everyday errands: texts, calls, emails, calendar events, notes, to-dos, reminders, alarms, sums, directions and searches. It shows you exactly what it's about to do and waits for you to confirm.

Start a line with a symbol to get there faster, like @ to text someone or + to add an event. Open it from the top of your app list, with a swipe down, as your phone's assistant, or with a slash from any app on a phone with a keyboard.

Most lines are understood right on your phone. With Premium ✦, you can also add your own key for Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini to help with the rest.

And much more

Thoughtful down to the details

Twenty-one widgets

Calendar, week, up next, weather, now playing, news, your Bluesky and Mastodon feeds, notes, people, folders, an alphabet app scroller and more. Any Android widget fits right beside them.

A glance hub

One line in the middle of the orbit shows what matters now: your next event, the weather, what's playing, or a message that just arrived.

Made for foldables

Open your phone to see two pages side by side, or give the big screen a layout, dock and theme of its own.

Easy to read, easy to reach

Large Print gives you high contrast, big buttons and every app named. Choose how quickly things move, or turn animation off. These are free for everyone.

Kids mode

A simple home screen with the apps you choose, big and clearly labeled, locked behind a PIN or pattern.

Keyboard phones

Start typing on the home screen to find an app, use hotkeys for everyday actions, and keep the home screen upright on slider phones.

Backups and shared layouts ✦

Save everything to one file for your next phone, or share a layout with a friend without any of your personal details in it. Restoring is free.

Works with your apps

“Add to Home screen” from Chrome, Maps or Contacts places the shortcut right on your page, and other apps' widgets can be pinned the same way.

Your home screen stays on your phone

Orbital doesn't need an account and doesn't include analytics, ads or crash reporters. Your layout, notes and contacts stay on your device. Permissions are asked for only when a feature needs them, and anything you decline simply stays off.

Read the privacy policy, which lists every request that can leave your phone and exactly what's in it.

Free and Premium

Free to use, with more when you want it

Orbital is free, and the free version is a complete launcher. Orbital Premium adds the rest of the themes, layouts and effects. Choose monthly, yearly with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time purchase that's yours for life.

Free

  • The orbit dock, flat row and container, on any edge
  • The dock over other apps
  • Five themes: Android, Orbital, Sleek, Classic and Large Print
  • Pages, with up to three
  • Orbital's own icon set, in each theme's own shape
  • Core widgets, the app drawer and edit mode
  • Ask Orbital, Kids mode and every accessibility setting

Premium ✦

  • All 25 themes, plus your phone's own colors
  • The Free roam and Drawer home layouts
  • Up to three orbits, and drawer mode for the dock
  • Your own accent color, glow, scan lines, pulse, flowing edges and Matrix
  • More pages, named tabs, and fade or flip page turns
  • Any icon shape and color style, including tiles, live tiles and icon packs
  • News, Feed, Now playing, Up next and Calculator widgets
  • Save backups and layouts to share
  • A separate layout for a foldable's big screen
  • Answers from Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini with your own key, and the Command prompt look

You can try any Premium theme on your own home screen for a minute before deciding.
