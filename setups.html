Home screens to try, each with its theme and layout. Open one on your Android phone and Orbital shows it to you first, so you can apply it or keep your own.

We're starting the gallery with a few setups of our own, and we'd love to add yours. See Share yours below.

    Setups marked ✦ use a theme or layout from Orbital Premium. Shared setups never include notes, contacts, calendar events or anything personal.

    Share yours

    Show off your home screen

    1. In Orbital, open Settings, go to Look & Feel and tap Share my setup, just under Save My Layout.
    2. Tap Get a code. You'll get a layout code starting with ORB- and a link anyone can open, plus a picture of your home screen to share wherever you like.
    3. To suggest it for this gallery, send us the code through the feedback form with a line about what makes your setup yours.
